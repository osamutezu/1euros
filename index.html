What counts as 1€?

A seller must have offered an identifiable unit for exactly €1 including tax, under pre-existing terms. Single objects, fixed bundles, selected bundles, promotions, and conditional prices can qualify. Negotiated reductions and arbitrary sums of independent prices do not.

Photographs of the object and its price are collected separately. Observations are linked to offers, not automatically counted as new offers.
